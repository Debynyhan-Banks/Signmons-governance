## How to read and close a row

Every row starts **Open**. Readiness describes source/evidence maturity, not acceptance. Relative size S/M/L/XL means small, bounded integration, substantial work, or material cross-layer/design uncertainty; confidence describes that estimate. These are provisional planning bands, not days. An XL row must resolve its smallest material design decision in its section card before coding; it is not permission to build a new subsystem or silently split the denominator.

Implementer owns mapping/implementation/tests and sanitized evidence; Debynyhan Banks owns acceptance. Account/environment owners approve consequential provider, deployment, secret, database, send, calendar, charge or restore actions separately. For each row, record source SHA, exact card/checklist, tested environment, evidence path, required commands/results, reviewer, acceptance date and any remaining limitations before changing Open to Accepted. A customer capability is not accepted by a passing fixture, helper, HTTP 200, READY, a commit, or document approval. Ticket closure still requires every adopted criterion, not only a ledger row.

Test-profile letters refer to the mandatory coverage/commands below. Source keys refer to the inspected source index. Each row also requires U (affected user surfaces) and X whenever external acceptance is needed. A full section card under `APP013_REMAINING_EXECUTION_CONTRACT.md` remains required before coding; this planning ledger does not invent a route, schema, policy threshold, recording permission or executable resource binding.
