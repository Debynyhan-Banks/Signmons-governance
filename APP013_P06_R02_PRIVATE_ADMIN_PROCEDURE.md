PostgreSQL's [ALTER ROLE warning](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/sql-alterrole.html#SQL-ALTERROLE-NOTES) also warns about plaintext SQL/history/server logs. TLS protects transport, not server-side logging. These are documentation findings, not a live compatibility test.
