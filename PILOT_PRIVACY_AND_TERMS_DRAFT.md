## Proposed Privacy Notice

### Who this covers

Eternity Mechanical Services LLC is the service business receiving your request. Signmons provides the digital intake and communications platform used for this pilot. Contact ben@eternityhvacr.com with questions about your request, messaging choices or privacy.

### Information used

For service requests, we collect information you provide, such as your name, contact information, service address and description of the issue. Where enabled and disclosed, we also process verification results, request and appointment status, communication content and delivery status, consent choices and security/audit records.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers or information unrelated to your service request in chat or text. Any approved payment flow uses the payment processor's secure interface rather than collecting card data in messages.

### Why information is used

Information is used to respond to requests, confirm details, apply the business's service-area and booking policies, coordinate approved appointments, provide requested updates, prevent misuse, honor communication preferences and investigate service or security problems. Automated assistance may help organize and respond to your request; it does not replace emergency services or a qualified technician's assessment.

### Service providers and sharing

Signmons and necessary communications, hosting, verification and payment providers may process information to perform their assigned services. Access must be limited to the service purpose. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or reasonably necessary to protect people, the service or legal rights, subject to applicable requirements.

We do not sell mobile numbers or SMS opt-in records, or share them with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS consent is specific to Eternity's declared messaging program and is not transferred to another business's campaign.

### Messaging choices

Appointment texting is optional. Message frequency varies with your request and appointments; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance, or contact ben@eternityhvacr.com. Declining appointment texts does not prevent requesting service. Email choices and any phone-access verification request are separate from appointment-SMS consent.

### Retention and security

Information should be retained only as needed for the stated service, security, consent/suppression, recordkeeping and legal purposes, with access limited to authorized parties. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security. A request to delete data may not erase records that must be retained for legal obligations, unresolved transactions, security or honoring an opt-out.

## Proposed Pilot Intake and Messaging Terms

### Service and parties

This supervised pilot lets you request service from Eternity Mechanical Services LLC using Signmons. Eternity is the service provider; Signmons supports intake and communications. The pilot is for Eternity's approved Cuyahoga County, Ohio service area and does not promise availability for every address or request.

### Request versus confirmed action

Provide accurate information and submit requests only when authorized. A draft or review reference is not a confirmed job, appointment, diagnosis or payment. Booking and any payment requirement must be explicitly presented and confirmed through the applicable approved workflow. Estimates, payment, cancellation and refund terms must be disclosed separately before the relevant commitment; these terms do not invent fees or waive customer rights.

### Automated assistance and safety

You may interact with an automated assistant. Confirm important details and contact the business if a response is unclear or incorrect. The assistant cannot inspect equipment or guarantee a technical diagnosis. Do not rely on this channel for emergency response. For immediate danger, call 911.

### Optional service-related SMS

If you actively opt in, Eternity may send texts about your service request, appointment confirmations, reminders, changes and cancellations. Marketing is not included. Consent is not required to request or purchase service. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to end this messaging program or HELP for assistance. You may receive a permitted confirmation of opting out, but further program messages must stop. Contact ben@eternityhvacr.com for help or to discuss another way to communicate. Carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages. Do not rely solely on text delivery for time-sensitive arrangements.

### Privacy and acceptable use

Review the linked Privacy Notice [approved public URL pending]. Do not impersonate another person, submit unauthorized personal information, abuse the system or attempt to bypass access controls. Service access may be restricted to address misuse or security risks consistent with applicable law.

### Questions and changes

Support and privacy contact: ben@eternityhvacr.com. Material changes to these terms require review and a published effective date. A change cannot silently enroll you in a new messaging purpose.
