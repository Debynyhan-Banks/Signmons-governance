## Owner-reviewed intelligence integration requirements

Dependencies: APP-019 full acceptance, reusing APP-017 rules and APP-018 behavior. Queued after memory and before appointment integrity/APP-033; not active. Original dashboard/failure criteria below remain mandatory.

- [ ] Conversation review and human corrections include safety/hallucination/failure categories, reviewer audit trail and resolution ownership.
- [ ] Prompt/model/policy/knowledge/tool versions and actual booking/payment/technician/job outcomes are linked without treating predictions as outcomes.
- [ ] Scenario sets separate synthetic unreviewed examples, development evaluation and held-out tests; related conversations/paraphrases remain in the same split with rights/retention and lineage controls.

Integration: existing audit/communications/customer/job records and quality surfaces. Cross-tenant review/export and leakage tests are mandatory. Preserve original metrics and full ticket DoD; optimization requires later measured evidence and explicit authorization.

## Ticket Summary

- Ticket ID: `APP-015`
- Repo: `signmons-calldesk-backend` (+ frontend app surface)
- Screen IDs: `SCR-APP-020`, `SCR-ADM-005`
- Objective: expose AI quality and failure diagnostics needed to operate a high-ticket, reliability-first dispatch platform.

## In Scope

- AI quality dashboard metrics:
  - repeat-question rate
  - first-response latency
  - escalation rate
  - intake failure rate
  - human handoff rate
- Admin failure review view for:
  - failed calls
  - failed intake transitions
  - failed webhook-linked outcomes
- Failure triage metadata and remediation status.

## Out of Scope

- Automated self-healing policy engine.
- Advanced model retraining pipeline.
- Full observability vendor replacement.

## Acceptance Criteria (must all pass)

- [ ] Quality dashboard displays required AI operational metrics.
- [ ] Failure review queue surfaces actionable incident details.
- [ ] Failures can be categorized and tracked to resolution.
- [ ] Tenant/admin access boundaries are enforced.
- [ ] Quality and failure events are auditable.
