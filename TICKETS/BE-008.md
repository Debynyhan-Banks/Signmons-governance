# BE-008 - Twilio Communications Foundation

## Ticket Summary

- Ticket ID: `BE-008`
- Repo: `signmons-calldesk-backend`
- Objective: provide the secure, provider-abstracted voice and SMS transport foundation used by CallDesk and every transactional notification workflow.
- Sequence: prerequisite/companion foundation for APP-013 and APP-033; it is not `Now` until the global pointer promotes it.

## In Scope

- Twilio inbound voice routing and authenticated webhook boundaries.
- Inbound/outbound SMS, missed-call text-back, transactional delivery, and provider status callbacks.
- Tenant phone-number ownership/configuration, forwarding/porting readiness, sender identity, and environment separation.
- Consent evidence, STOP/START/HELP handling, quiet-hour policy, suppression, idempotency, duplicate prevention, bounded retry, dead-letter visibility, and manual replay controls.
- Provider abstraction so business workflow state does not depend directly on Twilio identifiers.
- Privacy-safe communication history, delivery/failure status, cost/usage telemetry, monitoring, and audit events.
- Explicit recording/transcript configuration with notice, consent, retention, access, export, deletion, and redaction policy; disabled by default when policy is incomplete.

## Out of Scope

- OpenAI conversation behavior and booking orchestration, owned by APP-033.
- Marketing blasts, purchased contact lists, automated outbound campaigns, or unconsented messaging.
- Carrier, legal, or regulatory guarantees; applicable registration and tenant policy require evidence before activation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Every inbound webhook is signature-verified before tenant or workflow mutation.
- [x] Voice and SMS identities resolve to exactly one enabled tenant and environment or fail closed.
- [x] SMS consent, STOP/START/HELP, suppression, and quiet-hour rules are enforced server-side and auditable.
- [x] Message creation and provider callbacks are idempotent; retries cannot produce duplicate customer messages.
- [x] Failures have bounded retry, dead-letter visibility, ownership, and safe manual replay.
- [x] Provider identifiers and communication content are minimized and role-redacted in APIs, logs, and analytics.
- [x] Recording/transcript capture remains off until an approved policy and required notice/consent are configured.
- [x] Provider outage and degraded-mode behavior are tested without losing the canonical job, booking, payment, or notification event.

## Required Evidence

- Signature rejection, tenant-isolation, consent/opt-out, quiet-hour, idempotency, status-callback, retry/dead-letter, and provider-outage tests.
- Sandbox phone and SMS proof with redacted identifiers.
- Monitoring, rollback, environment-separation, and credential-rotation evidence.
