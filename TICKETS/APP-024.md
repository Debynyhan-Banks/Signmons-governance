# APP-024 - System Diagnostic Reports

## Ticket Summary

- Ticket ID: `APP-024`
- Repo: `signmons-calldesk-backend` (+ technician, tenant, and customer app surfaces)
- Screen IDs: `SCR-APP-033`, `SCR-TECH-006`, `SCR-CUST-008`
- Objective: turn verified field measurements and technician findings into tenant-scoped, professional diagnostic records that can be reviewed, delivered to the customer, and connected to follow-up work.
- Sequence: future Signmons Field work after the active Stripe and Twilio foundation and after the governed technician/pricebook prerequisites. This ticket is not `Now`.

## In Scope

- Tenant-managed, versioned diagnostic templates for HVAC, refrigeration, plumbing, electrical, and other approved trades.
- Technician mobile capture of equipment identity, measurements, units, observations, photos, recommendations, and signature/attestation.
- Deterministic, versioned calculations with retained source values; HVAC examples include temperature split, superheat, and subcooling when the configured template requires them.
- Draft, review, approved, delivered, superseded, and void report states with role-based transitions and audit history.
- Tenant-branded customer report with findings, approved recommendations, relevant photos, secure expiring view access, and PDF export.
- Links to the tenant-scoped job, customer, property, equipment history, estimate, invoice, and approved follow-up workflow.
- Delivery/failure status and safe retry through the governed communications foundation.
- AI assistance limited to completeness checks, contradiction flags, and plain-language drafting from retained verified inputs.

## Out of Scope

- Autonomous diagnosis, invented readings, or replacement of licensed technician judgment.
- Medical, engineering, code-compliance, warranty, or manufacturer-certification claims not explicitly supported by the tenant's approved workflow and evidence.
- Direct control of test instruments before an approved provider/device integration ticket exists.
- Public report URLs, cross-tenant templates/data, general-ledger accounting, or a per-report Signmons fee.
- Advanced predictive failure models or fleet-wide benchmarking in the Growth baseline; those remain Pro/Enterprise extensions.

## Plan Packaging

- Starter: notes, photos, equipment details, and simple completion summary.
- Growth: complete structured diagnostic capture, deterministic calculations, branded report, signature, secure customer delivery, and PDF export. Required for Growth general-availability claims.
- Pro: configurable multi-trade templates, supervisor quality-control workflow, equipment trends, and approved integrations.
- Enterprise: organization-wide template governance, multi-location controls, retention/compliance policy, and custom data pipelines.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every template, report, reading, calculation, revision, approval, and delivery event is tenant-scoped and auditable.
- [ ] Technician capture works on supported mobile widths and tolerates recoverable connectivity interruption without duplicate report versions.
- [ ] Units, source readings, formula version, result, author, and timestamps are retained for every calculated value.
- [ ] AI cannot create or alter authoritative measurements and cannot approve or independently assert a diagnosis.
- [ ] Required fields and contradictory readings fail closed before approval while preserving the draft for correction.
- [ ] Only authorized tenant roles can configure templates, review reports, or issue/supersede an approved report.
- [ ] Customer access exposes only the approved report through a private expiring credential and never exposes internal notes, hidden AI reasoning, or unrelated records.
- [ ] PDF and secure web representations contain the same approved version, tenant branding, equipment context, findings, recommendations, and delivery reference.
- [ ] Estimate/invoice conversion references the approved report without silently changing the report or its findings.
- [ ] Delivery failures do not destroy the approved report and provide an auditable safe-retry path.
- [ ] Starter, Growth, Pro, and Enterprise entitlements follow `OFFER_CATALOG.md` without per-report billing.

## Test and Evidence Plan

- Backend gates: `npm run -s build`, `npm test -- --runInBand`, `npm run -s arch:check`.
- Frontend gates: `npm run -s build`, `npm run -s lint`, `npm test -- --runInBand`.
- Governance gate: `node scripts/docs-consistency-check.mjs`.
- Contract proof: tenant isolation, RBAC, formula versioning, report state transitions, idempotent delivery, expiration, and cross-tenant denial.
- Browser proof: technician capture, tenant review, customer report, PDF parity, mobile accessibility, keyboard access, and failure/recovery states.
- AI safety proof: missing data, conflicting data, prompt injection in notes, attempted invented measurement, and unsupported diagnosis tests.

## Risks

- Incorrect measurements, units, or formulas could create unsafe or misleading reports.
- AI-generated wording could overstate technician findings or imply diagnostic certainty.
- Customer links or PDFs could expose private job, property, or equipment data.
- Configurable templates can create unmaintainable schema drift without versioning and validation.
- Diagnostic claims can create trade, licensing, warranty, insurance, and jurisdiction-specific obligations.
