# APP-034 — Acquisition-to-Profit Attribution

- **Repo:** Governance scope record; future implementation repository/interfaces to be assigned only when promoted through the canonical board.
- **Status:** Later; future governed scope only; not approved for implementation. No implementation, deployment, provider, billing, customer-data, or spend authority is granted.
- **Screen ID:** `SCR-APP-034` (future tenant outcome-report screen; inventory entry does not imply implementation).
- **Purpose:** Connect approved lead-source attribution to qualified opportunity, booking, completed job, collected customer revenue, governed job profitability, and retention so trades operators can measure business outcomes while preserving Revenue Integrity Dispatch.
- **Current source/evidence:** Backend BE-007 currently scopes reports to tenant jobs and reports created/booked/completed/cancelled counts, attributed/unattributed totals, lead-to-booking and booked-to-completed ratios, UTM source/medium/campaign, and landing pages. The source model primarily recognizes `website_chat`; current reporting does not establish collected revenue, profit, broad channel coverage, or retention. See `src/reporting/reporting.service.ts`, `src/reporting/reporting.controller.ts`, and `prisma/schema.prisma` in the backend repository.
- **Dependencies / promotion gates:** BE-007 foundation; APP-019 customer profiles/history; APP-020 estimates/invoices/payments/receipts and applicable APP-012 payment records; APP-026 job-costing/profitability; and relevant approved communication/channel foundations. Every dependency must be implemented and accepted before APP-034 is eligible for promotion. Promotion also requires the ordinary owner-approved canonical pointer/board transition; this ticket does not alter APP-013, WIP=1, or Next (`APP-017 -> APP-018 -> APP-019 -> APP-015 -> APP-016 -> APP-033`).
- **Scope:** Tenant-safe acquisition-source and lifecycle-outcome reporting; auditable source corrections; available campaign metadata; source-to-customer/job/invoice/payment lineage; separate estimates, invoices, collected payments, refunds/reversals, and (after APP-026) gross profit; approved manual/imported spend where later authorized; privacy-minimized summaries; and an outcome-focused `SCR-APP-034` screen.
- **Acceptance criteria (future, unchanged until separately governed):**
  1. Every query, record, join, correction, and export is tenant-scoped; negative cross-tenant tests deny access. Corrections retain actor, time, reason, before/after values, and provenance.
  2. Support a governed source model that may include `website_chat`, `web_form`, `phone`, `sms`, `google_business_profile`, `referral`, `property_manager`, `repeat_customer`, `manual`, and `unattributed`, with only technically and legally supportable channels enabled. Unknown source remains unattributed; no source is invented.
  3. Preserve available `landingPage`, `sourcePage`, `referrerHost`, `utmSource`, `utmMedium`, `utmCampaign`, `utmContent`, `utmTerm`, and approved click identifiers (`gclid`, `gbraid`, `wbraid`). No ad-platform integration is required for initial acceptance. If first-touch/latest-meaningful attribution is adopted, policy and correction history are explicit and versioned.
  4. Maintain stable tenant-owned lineage across lead/conversation -> customer -> booking -> job -> estimate -> invoice -> payment, including idempotent replay and visible correction handling.
  5. Distinguish estimates/quoted value, invoiced value, and collected revenue. Failed, partial, refunded, and reversed payments remain distinguishable. Never label estimates as revenue. Collected revenue is sourced from accepted APP-020 Money/payment records and applicable APP-012 payment state.
  6. Calculate gross profit only from accepted APP-026 governed job costs. Incomplete/missing cost data is explicitly unavailable, never silently zero. Define denominators, time basis, and missing-data rules for any cost-per-lead, booking conversion, acquisition cost, revenue/collected revenue by source, gross profit by source, ROAS (only with approved spend data), marketing contribution, or repeat-customer measure.
  7. Spend is manual or imported by an approved adapter. No autonomous ad buying, purchasing, campaign operation, or budget changes. Any ad-platform spend control requires separate explicit owner approval.
  8. Minimize personal data in analytics; exclude sensitive customer details and honor source-record retention/deletion policy.
  9. The screen shows sources, leads, bookings, completed jobs, collected revenue, gross profit only when valid, conversion rates, and unattributed volume. It is not a marketing campaign manager, website builder, SEO agency tool, social-media manager, or unrelated automation console.
- **Test/evidence plan when implementation is authorized:** tenant isolation/role denials; source normalization and unknown-source negatives; correction audit; lifecycle lineage and duplicate/replay; partial/failed/refund/reversal accounting; missing-cost, date-boundary and denominator cases; privacy output; approved adapter reconciliation/failure; and browser loading/empty/error/unavailable-profit/populated states. Produce correlated, tenant-safe source-to-payment/cost evidence and owner review.
- **Risks / exclusions:** Incorrect source joins or incomplete cost/payment coverage can misstate business outcomes. Clearly show coverage and uncertainty. Excludes autonomous campaigns/budget changes, generic SEO agency services, full website building, social-media management, broad unrelated marketing automation, and unsupported channel attribution. C.F. Mechanical may be a possible internal/design-partner validation environment only; no C.F.-specific behavior may be hard-coded. Generic workflows must remain usable across HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and adjacent trades.
- **Rollback / disabled state:** Until promoted and independently authorized, keep capability absent/disabled and do not expose a production route or collect new campaign identifiers. A future implementation must have a tenant-scoped feature disable/rollback plan and preserve immutable audit history for completed corrections.
- **Observable finish:** After future authorization, accepted evidence demonstrates tenant-safe source-to-lifecycle lineage, financially correct distinctions, cost-gated profit attribution, privacy controls, and owner-reviewed reporting. Documentation or tests alone are not customer capability acceptance.
