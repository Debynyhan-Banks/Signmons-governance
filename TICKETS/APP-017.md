Status: draft specification pending intelligence alignment adoption; not Now or authorized implementation. Screen: `SCR-APP-026`. Repo: signmons-calldesk-backend plus governed existing UI.

## Scope

Tenant-scoped versioned service areas, hours, urgency/escalation, eligibility, booking/payment requirements, approved fees, dispatch/after-hours handling and bounded automation triggers.

Reuse organization, coverage, payment and verification policies; do not duplicate approved services.

## Acceptance (all required after adoption)

Missing/inactive/conflicting rules fail closed; explicit no-payment policy is distinct from unknown. Every privileged change is role-checked and audited. Immutable version/price/currency snapshots survive retries and policy changes. Preview explains rules without performing actions. Cross-tenant, stale-policy, missing-fee and trigger-replay tests pass.

Before promotion, map each criterion to code, independent tests, actual environment evidence and reviewer; all criteria require full acceptance, not a pilot subset. Tenant boundaries, audit rollback, missing authority, retries and responsive existing-screen UI must be covered. No new route/API may be implemented without its contract mapping.

## Quality and exclusions

Backend build, lint, full tests, architecture and applicable Prisma/isolated database tests; affected UI build/lint/tests and responsive QA; governance consistency and release evidence. No production actions, real customer data, autonomous diagnosis, paid training, unapproved provider operations or standalone agent microservices.
