# APP-016 - Calendar Scheduling and Appointment Management

## Owner-reviewed dependency and financial integrity requirements

Dependencies: APP-015 full acceptance in the approved sequence; reuse accepted APP-011/012 and APP-013 recovery plus APP-017 policy. Not an instruction to recreate their implementation.

- [ ] Missing payment policy fails closed; explicitly not required is separate from unknown. Immutable price/currency/policy snapshots survive changes.
- [ ] Required verified server/webhook payment and live conflict recheck precede final promised booking/dispatch; a temporary hold or generated link is not success.
- [ ] Late payments, expired holds, duplicate requests/webhooks, concurrent slots and partial Calendar failures have a tested recoverable owner; model claims cannot override truth.

Existing full lifecycle, private-link, notification truth and recovery criteria below remain required. No actual production acceptance run is authorized by the test-plan wording; environment/release/participant approval must be explicit.

## Ticket Summary

- Ticket ID: `APP-016`
- Repo: `signmons-calldesk-backend` (+ frontend app and customer surfaces)
- Screen IDs: `SCR-APP-025`, `SCR-APP-015`, `SCR-APP-005`
- Objective: provide conflict-safe scheduling and a recoverable customer appointment lifecycle without duplicate jobs or calendar events.

## In Scope

- Tenant-scoped availability, booking rules, timezone and minimum-notice policy.
- Idempotent appointment confirmation after a live conflict recheck.
- Signed private customer link to view, reschedule or cancel an eligible appointment.
- Reschedule updates the existing event and reservation rather than creating a duplicate.
- Cancellation releases the database reservation and calendar event.
- Lifecycle events for exactly-once customer and internal notifications.
- Operator-visible exception and recovery states.

## Out of Scope

- Unapproved auto-dispatch to technicians.
- General calendar administration outside Signmons-created appointments.
- Marketing campaigns or promotional messaging.
- Raw payment-card handling.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Confirming a live slot creates one job reservation and one calendar event.
- [ ] The customer receives a private management action through the approved transactional channel.
- [ ] Viewing the management surface does not expose the credential in indexing, referrers, analytics or ordinary logs.
- [ ] Rescheduling rechecks the replacement slot and updates the existing event atomically or rolls back safely.
- [ ] Cancellation is idempotent and releases both calendar and database availability.
- [ ] Each completed lifecycle change emits one auditable internal event and one eligible customer notification.
- [ ] Notification failure never silently changes appointment truth and is recoverable by an authorized operator.
- [ ] Expired, malformed and cross-tenant credentials fail closed.

## Test and Evidence Plan

- Backend build, targeted lifecycle/idempotency tests and architecture check.
- Frontend build, lint and customer-management interaction tests.
- Labeled production acceptance: confirm, open link, reschedule and cancel while verifying one event and one notification at each stage.

## Risks

- Duplicate events under retries or concurrent slot selection.
- Timezone mismatch between customer display, tenant policy and calendar provider.
- Forwarded bearer links allowing unauthorized changes.
- Notification delivery being mistaken for appointment system-of-record truth.
