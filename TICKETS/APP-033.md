# APP-033 - OpenAI Voice Intake And Booking Orchestration

## Ticket Summary

- Ticket ID: `APP-033`
- Repo: `signmons-calldesk-backend` (+ tenant configuration and operator review surfaces)
- Objective: answer Twilio-delivered inbound calls with an OpenAI-powered conversational workflow that produces safe, confirmed, booking-ready records and governed handoffs.
- Sequence: requires the BE-008 communications foundation and may be promoted only by the global execution pointer.

## In Scope

- Low-latency conversational answering for tenant-owned inbound numbers with tenant greeting, brand voice, service facts, hours, service area, and escalation policy.
- Customer/property identification, service-request qualification, urgency and safety screening, structured summaries, and explicit confirmation of critical facts.
- Canonical SMS confirmation when tenant policy requires written confirmation; voice statements alone do not become authoritative critical data.
- Server-controlled handoff to scheduling, APP-012 payment policy, dispatch readiness, or a human owner/dispatcher.
- Human transfer, callback, voicemail, provider/model timeout, silence, interruption, unclear-input, and emergency fallback paths.
- Prompt/model/tool versioning, approved tenant knowledge sources, latency/error/cost telemetry, privacy-safe review, and auditable tool decisions.

## Out of Scope

- Autonomous emergency response, diagnosis, pricing exceptions, refunds, dispatch overrides, or unsupported promises.
- Invented availability, service coverage, pricing, credentials, measurements, warranties, or code/compliance claims.
- Sentiment scoring, broad transcription analytics, automated outbound sales campaigns, or replacing human escalation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A sandbox Twilio call reaches the approved OpenAI voice workflow and returns a tenant-correct greeting within the governed latency budget.
- [ ] The workflow safely creates or updates tenant-scoped conversation, customer, property, service request, and job records without duplicates.
- [ ] Critical fields are repeated/confirmed and, when required, sent through canonical SMS confirmation before booking readiness.
- [ ] Safety, emergency, unsupported-service, ambiguity, and policy exceptions fail closed to the correct human or emergency instruction.
- [ ] Model output cannot directly mark payment successful, confirm an unavailable appointment, assign a technician, or override server policy.
- [ ] Payment-required requests hand off to APP-012 without exposing provider identifiers or claiming success before webhook confirmation.
- [ ] Interruptions, silence, dropped calls, model/provider failure, and transfer failure preserve recoverable state and visible ownership.
- [ ] Tenant knowledge, prompts, tools, model versions, summaries, and decisions are versioned, access-controlled, and auditable.
- [ ] Recording/transcript behavior follows BE-008 policy and remains disabled when notice, consent, retention, or access rules are incomplete.

## Required Evidence

- End-to-end sandbox calls for ordinary, urgent, emergency, unsupported, ambiguous, payment-required, booking, transfer, dropped-call, and provider-failure scenarios.
- Duplicate/replay, tenant-isolation, prompt-injection, fabricated-availability, fabricated-price, policy-bypass, and sensitive-data leakage tests.
- Measured answer latency, turn latency, completion/fallback rate, cost, monitoring, rollback, and owner acceptance evidence.
